2. What is an Executive Summary?

An Executive Summary is a short text about a business. It gives simple and important information about the project.

الـ Executive Summary هو نص قصير عن المشروع. يعطي معلومات بسيطة ومهمة عن المشروع.

3. Questions and answers about a business

The model above helps you understand these questions and answers.

1. What is the business called?

Our business is called Easy Print.

2. What does it offer?

It offers a printing service.

3. Where is it?

It is in Algiers.

4. Who are the customers?

The customers are students and teachers.

5. How does it make money?

It makes money from printing and photocopying.

6. Lesson summary | خلاصة الدرس

An executive summary is a short description of a business. It tells us about the business idea, the product or service, the customers, and the source of money.

الـ Executive Summary هو وصف قصير للمشروع. يقدّم معلومات عن فكرة المشروع، المنتج أو الخدمة، الزبائن، ومصدر المال.
